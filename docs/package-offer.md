# Packaged Offer

## Lead Rescue Automation Sprint

Build a lightweight follow-up system for a local service business that is losing track of website forms, missed calls, quote requests, or social DMs.

## Scope

- Import or connect one primary lead source.
- Normalize lead fields: customer, source, request, estimate value, owner, stage, age, and next step.
- Add three to five risk rules for stale leads, quote delays, high-intent missed calls, and dormant prospects.
- Generate draft follow-up messages for email, SMS, or call notes.
- Build a daily queue for the owner or front desk.
- Add a weekly report for open leads, stale leads, booked value, and recovered opportunities.
- Record a short handoff walkthrough.

## Suggested Pilot Price

Suggested early pilot range: $500 to $1,500.

This is a judgment-call anchor for a first paid portfolio offer, not market data. The reasoning is that the scope is narrow enough to ship in a few focused days, low enough for a small business to approve without a long procurement cycle, and high enough to avoid turning custom workflow work into a token task.

## Timeline

Suggested timeline: three to five business days after the client provides access or exports.

This is a judgment-call estimate based on a narrow first version: one intake source, one queue, no complex CRM migration, and no custom billing/payment logic.

## Qualifying Questions

- Where do new leads currently arrive?
- Which leads are easiest to lose track of?
- What counts as a booked or won lead?
- Who owns follow-up today?
- What messages do you already send manually?
- Which tool should the team live in every day?

## Out of Scope for the Pilot

- Full CRM rebuild.
- Paid ad management.
- Payment processing.
- Complex two-way SMS compliance setup.
- Replacing existing staff workflow without a handoff period.
