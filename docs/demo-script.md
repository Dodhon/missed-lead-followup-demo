# Walkthrough Script

Use this as a short screen-recording script for freelance proposals.

## 60-Second Version

Hi, I built a small prototype for the exact kind of follow-up automation I would build for a local service business.

The dashboard starts with the only numbers an owner needs in the morning: open leads, at-risk value, booked value, and weighted pipeline.

The queue pulls every lead into one place, then flags the ones that need attention. For example, this HVAC quote is more than 24 hours old, so it moves into the at-risk queue and gets a clear next step.

When I open a lead, the system shows the owner, estimate size, lead age, probability, risk reason, and a ready-to-edit follow-up draft. The draft can switch between email, SMS, and call notes.

The implementation can be simple: a Google Sheet or Airtable base at first, then Gmail, forms, missed calls, or a CRM connection once the workflow is clear.

The goal is not to replace the business owner. The goal is to stop high-intent requests from sitting unseen, make follow-up ownership obvious, and give the team a daily recovery queue.

## Proposal CTA

If your leads already live in a spreadsheet, CRM, inbox, or form tool, I can adapt this workflow to your stack and ship a first working version quickly.
